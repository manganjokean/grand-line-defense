# Grand Line Defense

**Play online:** https://manganjokean.github.io/grand-line-defense/

A standalone, fan-made One Piece tower defense game. Open `index.html` in a browser—no installation, build step, or server required. All character portraits are original vector drawings included in the game, so the gameplay works offline (the optional Google Fonts fall back to system fonts).

## How to play

1. Choose a Straw Hat in the crew panel and click an empty grass tile to recruit them. The tan route must stay clear for the Marines.
2. Press **Start wave** and stop enemies from reaching the Sunny. Defeated Marines drop berries; clearing a wave earns a bonus.
3. Click a placed crewmate to see their attack range, upgrade them (up to level 3), or sell them. The nine Straw Hats each have signature animations: Luffy's stretching punch, Zoro's swords, Nami's Clima-Tact, Usopp's slingshot, Sanji's flaming kick, Chopper's hooves, Robin's giant hands, Franky's alternating robot punch and water blast, and Brook's umbrella sword.
4. Survive all **20 waves** across **four islands**: Shells Town Coast, Arlong Park, Water 7 Docks, and Marineford Harbor. A boss appears every fifth wave. When you sail to a new island, your crew's full investment is returned so you can reposition them on the new route.

**Shortcuts:** `1`–`9` select crew, `Esc` cancels selection, `Space` starts the next wave or pauses/resumes the battle. The buttons also let you pause and switch between 1× and 2× speed.
